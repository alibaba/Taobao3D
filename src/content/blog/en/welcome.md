## Why we started this blog

Papers capture rigorous methods, but a lot of valuable knowledge — the pitfalls, the engineering trade-offs, the failed experiments, the read on where things are heading — never fits into a paper. This blog is where we want to record those things and exchange ideas with the community.

## What you'll find here

- **Engineering practice**: hands-on lessons from our on-device 3D engine, real-time avatar driving, and more.
- **Research notes**: the motivation and intuition behind our published work, explained plainly.
- **Team perspectives**: our take on trends and technical directions in 3D/XR.

> We believe the best technical writing is reproducible, discussable, and open to challenge.

Follow along, and feel free to reach out via [GitHub](https://github.com/alibaba/Taobao3D).
